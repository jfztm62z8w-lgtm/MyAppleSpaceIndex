myAppleSpace

We're bringing the community back.

A long-running home for Apple users went quiet. We bought the address, and we're rebuilding it — for the people who made it worth visiting in the first place.

What this is

MyAppleSpace is a community for Apple users — a place for the small, specific groups that tend to form around this stuff: classic Mac collectors, Apple II tinkerers, iPhone and iPad power users, vintage hardware restorers, app and accessory enthusiasts, and anyone else who'd rather geek out with people who get it than scroll past it in a generic feed.

The idea is simple: smaller groups built around specific interests tend to produce better conversations than one giant general-purpose feed. We'd rather have a handful of focused, active corners than one noisy front page.

A note on what this isn't

This is a new project built on a domain that previously hosted a different community. It is not a continuation of, and has no operational connection to, the site that previously existed at this address — we're starting fresh, on our own terms.

MyAppleSpace is also not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Apple Inc. in any way. It's an independent, community-run space for people who use Apple products.
